1.3.1 - First Workshop release. Path of Exile style passive tree replaces level-up choices, endless procedural frontier with Mechanized/Mutated/Cursed nodes, oddity capstones and relics, shop tab (P / Back), co-op support, Mod Options settings.
1.4.0 - Frontier reveals now place tidy geometric patterns (7-14 nodes, no overlaps or crossing lines) that can curl back and link up with the tree. Fixes: tree no longer resets after reloading a saved run; co-op players can select the Skill Tree button.
1.5.0 - Red crits (crit chance past 100% rolls stronger crits). 13 new cluster patterns (6-25 nodes, loops, orbit exits that circle the tree). New clusters' stats follow your build, with a 'New direction' exit. Mechanized/Mutated levels now use Evolution and start at 0% chance. Minor Evolution/Curse nodes, named notables, Crimson Edge oddity and Blood Moon relic.
